Introduction

The Designer
Behind the Work

Born in Ecuador, shaped by
two cultures and a lifetime
of visual obsession.

Anabella Sauerbrey is a multidisciplinary fashion designer and creative director with a degree from Istituto Marangoni Milano — one of the world's most prestigious fashion institutions. Her work lives at the precise intersection of culture, identity, luxury, and innovation.


Rooted in her Ecuadorian heritage and trained in the epicentre of European fashion, Anabella brings a rare dual perspective to every project: the warmth of Latin American cultural storytelling combined with the rigour of Milanese fashion education. This duality is not a tension — it is her creative signature.


From hand-drawn sketches to AI-generated campaigns, from physical collections to complete digital brand ecosystems, she works across the full spectrum of the fashion creative process. Her experience spans fashion and accessory design, creative direction, branding, editorial design, content creation, and AI-driven visual production — always in service of the same core belief: that fashion is the most intimate form of cultural communication.

Selected Works

The
Collections

Each collection is a complete creative world — born from a concept, developed through research and experimentation, and realised through meticulous craft. Presented here are two defining chapters of Anabella's fashion design journey.

HAYA

Spring / Summer 2026

Concept

The Arabic and Swahili word for modesty and grace — HAYA explores the beauty of restraint, the architecture of the female body beneath fabric, and the silent power of covered form.

Materiality

Structured silks, fluid crepes, handwoven Ecuadorian textiles, and architectural organzas. A dialogue between rigidity and surrender, tradition and modernity.

Narrative

A meditation on cultural femininity — the intersection of heritage and aspiration. HAYA poses the question: what does a woman choose to reveal about herself, and what does she protect?

HAYA was conceived as both a garment collection and a philosophical statement. Inspired by the rich traditions of modesty dressing across cultures — from Andean textile heritage to the draped forms of the Arab world — the collection finds its voice in the space between exposure and concealment. The silhouettes are architectural yet fluid, the palette a study in warm neutrals punctuated by deep, earthy ochres and dusty roses that recall both the Ecuadorian highlands and the Saharan dusk.

I

Research & Moodboard

Ethnographic research, cultural textile archives, and visual reference curation across four continents.

II

Sketching & Concept

Hand-drawn development of silhouettes, draping studies, and proportion explorations across 200+ sketches.

III

Development

Fabric sourcing, toile construction, fit sessions, and AI-assisted visualisation for runway concept development.

IV

Final Collection

12-look collection finalised with technical specifications, runway styling direction, and brand communication.

TEALIA

Spring / Summer 2025

Concept

TEALIA takes its name from a botanical genus — a meditation on organic growth, cyclical nature, and the quiet intelligence of living systems applied to fashion construction.

Materiality

Bio-degradable linens, botanical-dyed silks, and experimental textile treatments that respond to light, movement, and the wearer's presence with an almost living quality.

Narrative

A study in emergence — how beauty and form arise from natural systems, and how fashion can embody that same organic intelligence while remaining unmistakably luxurious.

TEALIA is the creative thesis that announced her vision to the fashion world. Drawing on Ecuador's extraordinary biodiversity and the designer's lifelong relationship with the natural world, the collection translates botanical intelligence into wearable form. Silhouettes echo the unfurling of fern fronds, the cantilever of tropical leaves, and the precision of seed structures. The result is fashion that feels simultaneously ancient and radically new.
